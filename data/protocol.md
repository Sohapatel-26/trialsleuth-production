# Protocol ONC-301 (fictional)

A Phase 3, randomised, double-blind study of Veltrazumab versus placebo in adults with advanced non-small cell lung cancer. Sponsor: Halvern Therapeutics (fictional). Six sites: London, Bangalore, Boston, Madrid, Singapore, Sao Paulo. Version 4.0, effective 2026-03-01.

## 1. Visit schedule and allowed windows

Patients receive Veltrazumab or placebo by IV infusion every 21 days (one cycle). Required visits: Screening, Day 1, Day 8, Day 14, Day 21, then Day 1 of every cycle.

Pharmacokinetic (PK) blood draws must be taken at the scheduled time within these windows:
- Day 1 pre-dose draw: within 60 minutes before the infusion starts.
- Day 14 PK draw: scheduled time plus or minus 2 hours.
- Day 21 safety labs (blood count, liver and kidney function): within plus or minus 1 day.

A draw outside its window is a protocol deviation. The site must record it in the deviation log within 2 working days. Whether the patient's data stays in the PK analysis is decided by the Central Medical Monitor.

## 2. Investigational product storage

Veltrazumab vials must be stored between 2 and 8 degrees C, protected from light. Prepared infusion bags may be kept at room temperature (up to 25 degrees C) for at most 4 hours before the infusion starts.

Shipments travel with a temperature logger. Any reading outside 2 to 8 degrees C is a temperature excursion. The site must quarantine the affected vials (do not use, do not destroy), send the logger file to the sponsor within 24 hours, and wait for a written release or destruction decision.

## 3. Dosing and missed doses

Dose: 15 mg/kg IV over 60 minutes on Day 1 of each cycle. A cycle may be delayed by up to 7 days for logistical reasons without approval. A delay of 8 to 21 days needs Medical Monitor approval. A delay of more than 21 days means the patient stops study treatment.

Doses are never doubled to make up a missed dose.

## 4. Adverse event reporting

All adverse events (AEs) are recorded in the case report form within 5 working days. Serious adverse events (SAEs) must be reported to the sponsor's safety team within 24 hours of the site becoming aware, whatever the suspected cause. Grade 3 or higher infusion reactions are SAEs.

## 5. Withdrawal criteria

A patient must stop study treatment if they: withdraw consent, have confirmed disease progression, have a Grade 4 infusion reaction, become pregnant, or miss treatment for more than 21 days. A single protocol deviation alone is not a reason for withdrawal; the Medical Monitor decides case by case.

## 6. Deviation escalation

Site coordinators log every deviation and may request a ruling from the Central Medical Monitor. Rulings are binding for that case. Sites must document the ruling's case number in the patient file.
